What is Deutschelink?

Build your team in Kathmandu
without building the operation yourself.

Deutschelink helps international companies build dedicated teams in Kathmandu. Your team works from our professional office, works directly with you, and is supported locally by Deutschelink.

Your Git repos, your tools, your standup times. They function as a true in-house extension.
